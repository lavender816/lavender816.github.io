## Boundaries

Technical models and references remain upstream's educational approximations, not exact manufacturing floorplans or service drawings. Do not open a real power supply based on the virtual model. WebGL and sufficient GPU resources are required. No claim is made to reproduce proprietary internals more precisely than upstream.
